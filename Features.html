About Us

SheetEcodeS is a dynamic team of Bachelor of Science in Information Technology (BSIT) students who have come together to take on a challenging Capstone Project. Driven by passion, innovation, and a shared vision, we aim to apply our technical knowledge to create impactful software solutions that address real-world problems.

Our collaboration is rooted in the desire to improve our skills in teamwork, project management, and critical thinking. As we build a functional prototype, we’re also building our future careers—equipped with hands-on experience and the confidence to thrive in the IT industry.
